Junior Data Analyst

Apex Market Intelligence Ltd.
Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra (On-site)
Department: Retail & Consumer Insights
Salary: ₹6,00,000 - ₹8,50,000 PA

About Apex Market Intelligence

Apex Market Intelligence is a quantitative research firm advising retail brands on competitive pricing and merchandising trends. Our analytics platform tracks pricing signals across 50 million product SKUs in 12 global markets.

Role Description & Responsibilities

We are seeking an analytical and detail-oriented Junior Data Analyst to assist our consulting teams. You will aggregate structured datasets, run data quality audits, and develop weekly Tableau visualization dashboards for executive stakeholders.

  • Extract, transform, and clean structured transaction records from relational databases using SQL.
  • Build reproducible analytical workflows and exploratory statistical summaries using Python (pandas, matplotlib).
  • Maintain automated business intelligence dashboards and investigate discrepancies in weekly vendor data feeds.
  • Present quantitative insights during weekly cross-functional client progress calls.

Requirements & Skills

  • Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, Computer Applications, or related quantitative field.
  • 1-2 years of proven data analysis experience or internships in an analytical capacity.
  • Demonstrated fluency in SQL (joins, window functions, aggregations) and advanced Microsoft Excel.
  • Practical hands-on familiarity with Python or R for statistical data munging.
  • Working knowledge of BI tools such as Tableau, PowerBI, or Metabase.
